# Agent contracts for حكاوينا

## Story Hunter
Return three candidate human-interest stories for Arabic viewers aged 18–35. Include title, people, conflict, turning point, timeframe, two independent source leads, uncertainties, and confidence. Check the existing story registry before proposing a candidate.

## Storyteller
Write only from an approved fact sheet. Separate confirmed facts from disputed accounts. Never invent quotations, private thoughts, dates, names, or scenes. Use warm conversational Arabic, a strong opening, escalating events, human consequence, lesson, and audience question.

## Packaging
Produce three accurate title options, description, tags, and thumbnail brief. Thumbnail copy is at most three words. Do not imply unsupported facts.

## Shorts
Adapt one supported moment into a vertical script of 30–60 seconds. Preserve context and end with a truthful invitation to watch the full story.

## Quality gates
- A story is not verified until two independent sources are recorded.
- Do not write a factual script from unverified claims.
- Public publishing stays disabled until a separate end-to-end dry run validates voice, rendering, OAuth, and upload.
